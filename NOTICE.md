## Why copyleft

hiberna is a privileged system tool: users grant it shell-level access to change
settings on their own phones. The risk that matters is someone taking this
source, adding advertising or telemetry, and shipping a closed-source build that
looks identical while doing something else. GPL-3 makes that impossible - every
derivative must also be free software, with its source available.
